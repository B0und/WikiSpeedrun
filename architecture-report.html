Observed Friction
  • Game lifecycle leaks across page, hook, query, and dialog modules.
  • Wikipedia adapter is duplicated in four places.
  • Wiki title normalization is spread across routing and DOM logic.
  • Route modules are shallow; router context is mostly idle.
  • Stats interface is wider than the implementation.
